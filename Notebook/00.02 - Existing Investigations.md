I'm not the first person to investigate the Samsung NX protocol. Some people have done things such as control the aperture or focus of a lens. I've not found enough information that would let someone create a protocol adapter, either to allow Samsung lenses to be used on other types of camera, or the other lenses to be used on a Samsung camera.

My goal is to understand the protocol well enough that I can fit a non-Samsung electronic lens to a Samsung camera and have the focus/aperture/OIS work.
