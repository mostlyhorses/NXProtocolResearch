The lens often responds to the camera with a packet that has an 0x2xxx code after the length. These codes are not described in the lens information table, so this is based on observations.

# 2001 - OK

## Data

There is often data following the code. When the packet is in response to an operation by the camera it is obvious what the data relates to, but sometimes the lens responds with the 2001 code to a poll or issues it as a status. In the latter cases its difficult to know how to interpret the data.

Examples of data following poll/status:

- 0B 20 01 01 01 6E 01 75 0E FF 00 (18-55mm lens)
- 0B 20 01 01 01 84 01 75 0E FF 00 (18-55mm lens)
- 0B 20 01 0F 0C FF 04 E8 0F 5E 03 (20-50mm lens)
- 0B 20 01 01 04 F8 04 E8 0F 5E 03 (20-50mm lens)
- 04 20 01 01
- 04 20 01 00
