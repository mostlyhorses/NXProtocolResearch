# CPUs

Service manual for 12-24mm lens lists PIC24EP128GP206 as the main CPU.

Other lenses appear to use a custom samsung part, NXL1012-4.

The samsung cpu name is suspiciously the same as the 30mm lens model number XL1012

Some lenses have multiple microcontrollers looking after different functions, which will be why the firmware lists multiple version numbers.

Microchip offers a service where you can get custom markings. https://www.microchipdirect.com/faq-main?catid=1c99a89d-722f-69fd-9699-ff0000260584&baseID=b0e4b29d-722f-69fd-9699-ff0000260584 . Perhaps all the parts are microchip PICs ?

NX1 uses an auxiliary 8-bit micro mc97f2664 from ABOV Semi (korean).

# STM32 SPI Glitches

Had annoying bug where things work work depending on the load on the SPI pins, e.g. scope was sometimes ok, logic analyser not ok. Appears that the default 'very high' drive strength on the MISO line was causing glitches on the SCK line, which then caused the data to be shifted out badly. What it looked like was the first byte went ok (response to the 0x03AA55) but the second and third were zero. I think the AA and 55 were being shifted out on glitches caused by the edge of the 0x03.

Changed to low speed for the SPI and other GPIO, and also added filters (10R + 1n) on lines from the camera (SCK, MOSI, BODY_POLL).

Comms seems reliable at 250kHz, but not at 2MHz. Can set the Standard/Fast Comms parameters both to 250.
